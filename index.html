01 09/2024 – Present

EnerjiVeri: 100 Years of Turkish Energy Data

TÜBİTAK 1001 project No. 323K520, sole web developer

A hundred years of Turkish energy production, consumption and policy data was spread across CSV files and PDFs. I built the project's Turkish-language portal that brings it together, with time-series charts, sector breakdowns and interactive dashboards.

The portal is live and used by the project's researchers and policy stakeholders ("Energy Strategies in the Second Century of the Republic").

02 2024 – 2025

Tune2Travel: Music Videos and Destination Marketing

TÜBİTAK 1001 project, data infrastructure and NLP

The project studies how music videos shape the way people see a travel destination, which needs a lot of multilingual social media data. I built the data side: collecting videos and comments through the YouTube API, the ETL pipelines, and the NLP and ML models that pull cultural and sociological signals out of the comments.

Marketing and sociology researchers on the project use it to compare patterns across thousands of music videos in several languages.

Built with

  • Python
  • NLP
  • spaCy
  • Gensim
  • YouTube API
  • ETL
  • Selenium

03 2024 – 2025

OSINT-Driven SCADA Threat Discovery

Field assessment on a SCADA testbed in Milan

Power distribution operators have no structured way to combine open-source intelligence about their assets with AI-based protection. I did a hands-on OSINT vulnerability assessment of a SCADA testbed in Milan and worked on a framework that pairs OSINT-based threat discovery with AI-based protective controls.

Published at IEEE UPEC 2025.

Under review Turkish Journal of Neurology

Comparative Performance of the Mini-Mental State Examination and the Montreal Cognitive Assessment at Their Conventional Thresholds

Özgenç, G., Biricik, M., Kırbaş, D.

Single-centre retrospective audit comparing the MMSE and the MoCA through paired discordance (exact McNemar test) and ROC analysis (DeLong's test). The MoCA's higher sensitivity for mild cognitive impairment comes from its less strict conventional threshold, not from better discriminative validity.
