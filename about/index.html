About

About me

Cybersecurity researcher and Erasmus Mundus scholar in the CyberMACS MSc in Applied Cybersecurity, with hands-on experience in network administration, system deployment and threat discovery. I have run critical IT infrastructure and software rollouts under pressure, and I want to specialise in Zero Trust architecture, cloud security and SASE.

Most of my day-to-day work is systems work: keeping research infrastructure running and writing the tools and data pipelines the projects need. Some of it is applied machine learning on power grid and EEG data.

  • OSINT-driven threat discovery
  • Anomaly detection in critical infrastructure

  • Zero Trust, cloud security, SASE
